Salmon Dip and Roasted Seaweed

Ingredients

  • 1 4oz salmon fillet
  • 1 Avocado
  • 1 cup of microwaveable rice
  • Gyoza sauce (nonspecific amount)
  • Spicy Mayo (nonspecific amount)
  • Roasted Seaweed strips

Instructions

  1. Airfry 1 salmon fillet 10 minutes at 400F.
  2. Microwave cup of rice for 55 seconds.
  3. Mix avocado and rice in a bowl while salmon cooks.
  4. Add gyoza sauce and spicy mayo to your rice and avocado mix.
  5. Add cooked salmon to avocado and rice and mix.
  6. Use roasted seaweed as wrap to eat salmon dip and enjoy!

This makes one serving!
