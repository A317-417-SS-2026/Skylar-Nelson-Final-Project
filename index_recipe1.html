Avocado and Hardboiled Egg on Hashbrown

Ingredients

  • 2 Frozen hashbrown patties
  • 1 Avocado
  • 1 Hardboiled Egg

Instructions

  1. Airfry 2 hashbrown patties for 10 minutes at 400F.
  2. Once cooked, spread avocado on both patties.
  3. Peel and cut a hardboiled egg into four parts.
  4. Assemble and enjoy!

This makes one serving!
