Meat Pasta

Ingredients

  • 1lbs of ground beef
  • 1/2 jar of meat sauce
  • 1 box of Kraft mac and cheese
  • 1 can of corn
  • Onion powder
  • Paprika powder
  • Garlic Powder
  • Salt

Instructions

  1. Boil a pot of water, salt to taste.
  2. Brown 1lb of ground beef in a another pot and season to desired taste.
  3. Once water is boiling, dump pasta noodles into pot and let boil for 8 minutes.
  4. At 3 minutes, add a can of corn to boiling pot of pasta.
  5. Drain pasta and corn.
  6. Add half a jar of meat sauce to the pot of ground beef.
  7. Add cheese powder packet to the meat sauce.
  8. Combine the two pots, mix throughly and enjoy!

This makes four servings!
